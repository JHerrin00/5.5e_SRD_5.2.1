# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-11

First release, built from the SRD 5.2.1 PDF.

### Added

- `core_rules/`: the rules text of each chapter
- `classes/` and `subclasses/`: 12 classes and their 12 subclasses
- `backgrounds/` and `species/`: 4 backgrounds and 9 species
- `feats/`: 17 feats
- `spells/`: 339 spells
- `magic_items/`: 258 magic items
- `monsters/` and `animals/`: 235 monsters and 95 animals
- `fantasy_statblocks/`: every monster and animal as a Fantasy Statblocks note, plus an optional 2024-style layout
- `.build/03_json/`: one JSON file per category
